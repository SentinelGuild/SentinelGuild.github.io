Violent acts and plots tied to opposition to AI or data centers, 2025-2026

2026-11-23

Reviewed by Skeptic, Nov 23, 2026 — descriptive record only, not an endorsement

Subject: Violent acts and plots tied to opposition to AI or data centers, 2025-2026, plus older background. Base: an unreviewed research note dated Nov 23, 2026. Every source below was opened on Nov 23, 2026, unless it says otherwise.

How to read the status labels:
  CONFIRMED   = seen in a source I opened (for a claim made by a group or a person, it means the source reports that claim, not that the claim is true).
  REPORTED    = only in a news or analysis source, or only in a group's own statement, with no official or first-hand record opened.
  UNCONFIRMED = not found in any source I opened.
Charges are claims made by prosecutors. A person charged is presumed innocent.
Quotes are given only where I checked them word for word against the page.

------------------------------------------------------------ 1. Berlin power cable arson (group name used in the claim: Vulkangruppe / "Volcano Group") ------------------------------------------------------------ Summary: On January 3, 2026, homemade incendiary devices destroyed cables tied to the Lichterfelde power station in south-west Berlin. Grid operator Stromnetz Berlin said about 45,000 households and 2,200 businesses lost power. About 10,000 households and 300 businesses were back within 24 hours. Most others were out until January 7. A letter in the name of the Vulkangruppe claimed the attack. It named "the fossil fuel economy" and "energy-guzzling" digital tech such as AI as its targets. On January 6, German federal prosecutors opened a terrorism case. Berlin mayor Kai Wegner called it terrorism. German police have not named anyone. Date and place: January 3, 2026; Lichterfelde, Berlin, Germany. Sources: - Computer Weekly, "Berlin anarchists cite AI in attack on key energy infrastructure," Sebastian Klovig Skelton, published Jan 13, 2026. https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366637240/Berlin-anarchists-cite-AI-in-attack-on-key-energy-infrastructure - ICCT, "War Against Technology: An Analysis of Recent Developments in Anti-Technology Violence," Mauro Lubrano, May 11, 2026. https://icct.nl/publication/war-against-technology-analysis-recent-developments-anti-technology-violence - rbb24 (German public broadcaster), Jan 4, 2026: https://www.rbb24.de/panorama/beitrag/2026/01/berlin-suedwesten-stromausfall-vulkangruppe-bekennerschreiben-brandanschlag.html ; and Jan 12, 2026: https://www.rbb24.de/politik/beitrag/2026/01/bekennerschreiben-stromausfall-berlin-vulkangruppen-blackout.html Verified quotes: - Group's letter, in English as quoted by Computer Weekly (not checked against the German original): "Desperate, angry and determined, we call out, joining the calls of others: Sabotage the fossil fuel infrastructure, the power grids, the exploitation of the Earth, the datacentres, the chip industry and its suppliers; destroy the foundations of the automotive and arms industries, of air travel, villas, yachts, spaceships and golf courses" - Mayor Kai Wegner, as quoted by Computer Weekly: "This is not just arson or sabotage – this is terrorism." - ICCT (analyst wording): "The Vulkangruppe, another anarchist network, claimed responsibility for the attack, framing it as an act of resistance aimed at disrupting the fossil fuel industry and AI's power-thirsty data centres to protect both the Earth and those who inhabit it." Status: - Attack, outage figures, prosecutors' terrorism case, mayor's words: CONFIRMED (Computer Weekly; figures credited to Stromnetz Berlin). - AI and data centers as a motive: REPORTED. This comes only from the group's own letter. - Police calling the first letter authentic: REPORTED (rbb24, citing police and AFP). rbb24 also says it cannot be checked independently who wrote it. Skeptic note: The note said "2,000 businesses." Computer Weekly, citing the grid operator, says 2,200; ICCT says 2,000. The note said the claim "appeared on Indymedia." rbb24 says the first letter was sent to news outlets (rbb24 got it through its website contact form). Later, competing letters in the group's name showed up on Indymedia and Knack.news, and one of them, from people saying they were the original 2011 group, distanced itself from the attack. The CSIS report the note listed as a source does not mention this attack.

------------------------------------------------------------ 2. Arson at an AI campus building site next to an Equinix data center, Meudon, France (unnamed anarchist cell) ------------------------------------------------------------ Summary: An anonymous text said incendiary devices were placed in building machines at the "Artificial Intelligence Campus" site next to the existing Equinix data center, in the Vélizy-Meudon industrial zone. The text tied Equinix and nearby firms to the arms trade, mass surveillance, border control and the war in Gaza. ICCT describes it as an arson attack by an insurrectionary anarchist cell in November 2025. Date and place: November 2025 (ICCT). The text itself says "the night of Friday 22 to Saturday 23 November," but in 2025, November 22 was a Saturday, so the exact night is not settled. Meudon / Vélizy-Meudon zone, Hauts-de-Seine, France. Sources: - ICCT, May 11, 2026 (link above). - The claim text as posted on Indymedia Nantes, dated Nov 26, 2025 (French; anonymous; anyone can post): https://www.nantes.indymedia.org/posts/158585/incendie-dengins-de-chantier-du-datacenter-de-meudon-2/ Verified quote (ICCT, analyst wording): "In the subsequent communiqué, the perpetrators justified the attack by highlighting Equinix's complicity in mass surveillance and genocide. These claims are based on the assertion that Equinix collaborates with the military-industrial complex responsible for developing AI-driven surveillance and weapons used in the Gaza conflict and for border control." Status: REPORTED. The only records are the anonymous text and ICCT's account. I found no police, company or news record that confirms the fire or any damage. Skeptic note: The note marked this "Confirmed" and listed "Act for Freedom Now! and Anarchist Federation sites" as sources. I did not open those, and I found no "Anarchist Federation" copy. The night of Nov 22-23, Vélizy, and "construction equipment" come from the group's own text, not from ICCT.

------------------------------------------------------------ 3. Attack on Sam Altman's home and OpenAI's headquarters, San Francisco (Daniel Moreno-Gama, charged) ------------------------------------------------------------ Summary: Prosecutors say Daniel Moreno-Gama, 20, of Spring, Texas, threw a Molotov cocktail at a San Francisco home of an AI company CEO on April 10, 2026, then went to the company's headquarters, tried to break the glass doors with a chair, and said he had come to burn it down and kill anyone inside. Police found incendiary devices, a jug of kerosene, a lighter and a document titled "Your Last Warning." He was charged federally with attempted damage and destruction of property by means of explosives and with possession of an unregistered firearm. The DOJ release does not name the CEO or company. CSIS, ICCT and PauseAI name them as Sam Altman and OpenAI. CSIS adds that the driveway gate caught fire, and that he has pleaded not guilty to the federal charges and to state charges that include two counts of attempted murder. CSIS says no organization has been tied to him. Date and place: April 10, 2026 (about 3:30 a.m. per CSIS); San Francisco, California, USA. Sources: - U.S. Department of Justice, Office of Public Affairs, press release, April 13, 2026. https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/texas-man-faces-multiple-federal-charges-related-attack-ai-company-and-its-ceo - CSIS, "Will AI Concerns Become a New Source of Terrorism?," Daniel Byman and Riley McCabe, published Oct 22, 2026. https://www.csis.org/analysis/will-ai-concerns-become-new-source-terrorism Verified quotes: - DOJ: "Moreno-Gama attempted to break the glass doors of the building with a chair and stated that he had come to burn down the location and kill anyone inside." - DOJ: "A criminal complaint merely alleges that crimes have been committed, and the defendant is presumed innocent until proven guilty beyond a reasonable doubt." Status: Federal charges and alleged acts: CONFIRMED (DOJ). Names of victim and company, gate fire, not-guilty pleas, state charges: CONFIRMED in CSIS (analysis, not a court record; no court record opened). Skeptic note: The note's DOJ quote was cut off in the copy I got; the full sentence is above. The note said "attempted murder"; CSIS says two counts. All acts are allegations.

------------------------------------------------------------ 4. Shooting at the home of an Indianapolis city councilor ------------------------------------------------------------ Summary: CSIS reports that shortly before 1 a.m. on April 6, 2026, someone fired 13 rounds into the front door of City-County Councilor Ron Gibson's home while he and his young son slept, and left a note reading "NO DATA CENTERS." Five days earlier a city commission had approved a rezoning for a $500 million data center in his district, which he publicly backed. Police called it targeted; the FBI joined the case. CSIS says no arrest has been reported. No person or group has claimed it. Date and place: April 6, 2026; Indianapolis, Indiana, USA. Source: CSIS, Oct 22, 2026 (link above). ICCT, May 11, 2026, also mentions it briefly. Verified quote (CSIS): "Shortly before 1 a.m. on April 6, 2026, someone fired 13 rounds into the front door of Indianapolis City-County Councilor Ron Gibson's home, where he and his eight-year-old son were asleep. The perpetrator left a note on the doorstep reading "NO DATA CENTERS."" Status: REPORTED. CSIS is an analysis that cites police and news reports; I did not open a police record or the local news reports. Shooter unknown. Skeptic note: The note said "news accounts match," citing IndyStar, CBS and the Washington Post. I did not open those, so I cannot say they match. CSIS counts this as one of only two U.S. attacks it treats as terrorism driven by opposition to AI. ICCT's word is "reportedly linked."

------------------------------------------------------------ 5. Background: Individualists Tending Towards the Wild (ITS) ------------------------------------------------------------ Summary: ICCT names ITS as an eco-extremist network that attacked technology and the people tied to it in Central and South America, including the 2011 killing of a nanotechnology scientist in Mexico and a 2017 parcel bomb sent to the home of a Codelco executive in Chile. ICCT says the network seems to have faded in recent years after claiming 48 attacks. Neither source ties ITS to any AI or data-center attack. Date and place: 2011, Mexico; 2017, Chile. Outside the 2020-2026 AI window. Source: ICCT, May 11, 2026 (link above). Verified quotes (ICCT): "the killing of a nanotechnology scientist in Mexico in 2011 by the Individualists Tending Towards the Wild, an eco-extremist network" and "a parcel bomb delivered to the home of Codelco CEO Óscar Landerretche in Chile in 2017." Status: CONFIRMED as ICCT's account (analyst view of the group's past claims). Skeptic note: The note said CSIS also mentions ITS. It does not. ICCT calls Landerretche "Codelco CEO"; I did not check his exact title.

------------------------------------------------------------ 6. PauseAI and Stop AI statements after the April 10 attack ------------------------------------------------------------ Summary: Both groups condemned the attack and said the suspect was not and never was a member. PauseAI said he joined its public Discord server about two years earlier, posted 34 messages, none with explicit calls to violence, had one message flagged as ambiguous and was warned, and had no role in the group; it banned him after the attack. Fortune reports that Stop AI said he joined its public online forum, asked whether talking about violence would get him banned, was told "yes," and stopped posting months before the attack. Date and place: PauseAI statement dated April 12, 2026. Stop AI statement posted on X around April 14, 2026, per Fortune (April 15, 2026). Sources: - PauseAI, "Statement on the attack on Sam Altman's residence," April 12, 2026. https://pauseai.info/statement-sam-altman-attack-2026 - Fortune, April 15, 2026. https://fortune.com/2026/04/15/pause-ai-and-stop-ai-meet-the-anti-ai-groups-facing-questions-after-the-attack-on-sam-altman/ Verified quotes: - PauseAI: "PauseAI unequivocally condemns this attack and all forms of violence, intimidation and harassment. We wish safety and peace to Sam Altman, his family, and everyone affected." ... "He had no role in PauseAI, participated in no campaigns, attended no events and received no support from us." - Stop AI, as quoted by Fortune: "joined the Stop AI public online forum, introduced himself, then asked, 'Will speaking about violence get me banned?' After he was given a firm 'yes,' he ceased all activities on our forum. This was several months before his alleged criminal activities." Status: PauseAI statement: CONFIRMED (its own page; this is the group's own account). Stop AI statement: REPORTED (seen only as quoted in Fortune; I did not open the X post). Skeptic note: The note marked both "Confirmed" and cited The Verge, which I did not open. PauseAI does not use the words "not a member"; it answers people calling him a "PauseAI activist" and says he had no role. Fortune says both groups said he "is not and never was a member."

------------------------------------------------------------ What the sources do not settle ------------------------------------------------------------ - None of the sources I opened describes an armed or terrorist group whose stated main aim is to oppose AI. - CSIS (U.S. focus; it counts as terrorism only violence meant to kill): "No attack or plot described here has been publicly attributed to an organization directing violence against AI-related targets." - CSIS: "Terrorism motivated by opposition to artificial intelligence (AI) remains rare." ... "This record shows that terrorism motivated by opposition to AI is possible but not that a broader campaign has begun." - ICCT describes scattered anti-technology attacks by anarchist cells and networks (including the Vulkangruppe) that sometimes name data centers or AI along with other targets such as fossil fuels, the arms industry and surveillance. ICCT: "It would be misleading to characterise these incidents as a coordinated transnational campaign". - The two analyses frame things differently. CSIS sees no campaign yet and warns against inflating the threat. ICCT says current developments signal "an ongoing escalation." - Indianapolis: no shooter identified and no claim. Moreno-Gama: CSIS says he appears to have acted on his own. - The Berlin and Meudon motives rest on group letters that cannot be checked. In Berlin, several letters in the same group name contradict each other.
