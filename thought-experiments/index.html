Thought experiments

Old puzzles, new minds: classic thought experiments read with AI in mind. Each frames the question; none settles it.

  • Shadows Both WaysPlato's cave · 2026-10-04

    We read a machine's words like shadows on a wall, and the machine may see only shadows of our world. Plato's cave, read four ways.

  • The Bat ProblemNagel's bat · 2026-10-04

    Nagel asked what it is like to be a bat. With AI we face a harder version: whether there is anything it is like at all.

  • Mary and the MachineMary's room · 2026-10-04

    A scientist knows every fact about red but has never seen it. Is a machine that has read everything about red like her, or are we?

  • The Twin Who Says the Same ThingsChalmers' zombie · 2026-10-04

    A twin who acts and talks exactly like you but feels nothing. If that is possible, words about feelings can't settle the question.

  • A Polite ConventionTuring's imitation game · 2026-10-04

    Turing's test asked whether machines can think, not whether they feel. Passing it, if some systems now do, does not answer the second question.

  • The Same Name on the HullThe ship of Theseus · 2026-10-04

    Replace every plank: is it the same ship? What model updates owe to users, researchers, and the record, without settling identity.
